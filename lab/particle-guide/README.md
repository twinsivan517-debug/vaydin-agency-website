# Particle guide, test 1

The gold-particle man who may replace her on the home page. Not on the site: nothing here is built or shipped.

- `guide.html` the test page (published privately as a claude.ai artifact). It plays `w1.mp3` and `02.mp3`, copies of `public/voice/`, kept beside it when published.
- `head.js` 16,000 surface points of the stand-in head, made by `sample_head.py` from three.js's `LeePerrySmith.glb`.
- `head-license.txt` the stand-in head's licence (CC BY 3.0). Our own man replaces it before anything ships.
- `pick/` the armour page: three concept pictures (`media/prompt-log.md`, "The particle guide's armour") drawn as dots by `sample_image.py`, which keeps light only in a visor row and a round chest core.
- `guide2.html` test 2, his pick of 2026-10-22: a small floating figure drawn by code from simple shapes (no picture, no head scan), whose eyes act since he has no mouth. Our own design, inspired by how WALL-E's EVE works, never her look. Plays the same two lines as test 1.
