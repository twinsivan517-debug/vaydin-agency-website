THE MYSTERY SHOP · THREE DOORS

Your patients test you every day.
Now someone reports the results.

Every new patient walks through one of three doors and forms a verdict long before anyone greets them. We walk through all three, the same way, unannounced. Every 30 to 60 days, on a day nobody in the clinic can predict.

DOOR 01

The message

We write to your clinic as a patient, on WhatsApp, because that's where Dubai books. We ask what real patients ask: the treatment, the price, the availability. Then we record what your clinic said, and when.

DOOR 02

The profile

Before messaging, a patient has already seen your Google profile, your rating, your latest reviews, your Instagram. We review what they see: the verdict formed before you ever knew they existed.

DOOR 03

The follow-through

Most clinics answer once. The real test is what happens after: was a booking offered, did anyone follow up, what happened when we went quiet. That's where revenue leaks, silently.
