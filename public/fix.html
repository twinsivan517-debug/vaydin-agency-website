HOW WE CONNECT — AND WHAT WE NEVER TOUCH

You grant access. You keep the keys.

No passwords, no software installed at the clinic, and every access revocable by you in one click.

What we ask for

  • ✓WhatsApp — your number runs on WhatsApp Business infrastructure; handed back whole if we ever part
  • ✓Instagram — partner access through Meta Business Manager, the official delegation
  • ✓Google — you invite us as a profile manager; one click to revoke
  • ✓Email — forwarding or delegated access to the inquiries inbox only
  • ✓Website — editor access for the concierge changes

What we never touch

  • ✗Your patient records — your medical system stays yours; we never import it
  • ✗Your passwords — every connection is official delegation, never a shared login
  • ✗Clinical conversations — anything medical routes to your team, always
  • ✗Your money — payments stay between you and your patients
  • ✗Your ad accounts' billing — spend is paid direct, never marked up
We keep only the conversations we handle and the bookings we confirm — encrypted, minimal, and deleted on schedule. The full access list is written into your agreement, item by item.
