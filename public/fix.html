THE ENGINE · SIX THINGS, FROM MESSAGE TO VISIT

From the first message to the visit.

Swap through the six. Nothing to install at the clinic: the engine lives where your patients already are.

◈ THE ENGINE · ILLUSTRATION
ENGINE 01 / 06

Every first message gets your menu

A patient who starts a chat at 2am gets your clinic's menu at 2am: book, treatments, where you are, offers, a person, and a consultation if you offer one. Every answer is wording your clinic approved, and a price is only ever the one you set. Anything typed instead goes straight to your reception, and the patient is told a person will reply.

✗Without: the 2am message waits until morning, if it's ever seen
✓With: your menu at once, and anything else handed to your team
ENGINE 02 / 06

Booked inside the chat

The patient taps a treatment, picks a time, and is booked and told in the same chat. Your reception is told at once, with one button if the time has to move. If you prefer, reception confirms every booking first.

✗Without: "let me check and get back to you", and nobody does
✓With: the time the patient picked is the booking, in the same chat
ENGINE 03 / 06

Reminders before each booked visit

A confirmation when they book, then a reminder a day before and another two hours before, or at the times you choose. Each reminder carries two buttons: Confirm and Reschedule. Reschedule shows new times, and the old one is freed the moment a new one is picked. Nothing the clinic starts is sent at night.

✗Without: empty chair, blocked doctor hour, no warning
✓With: the patient who can't come moves the visit in two taps
ENGINE 04 / 06

The quiet list, invited back

The patients who came once and drifted. You give us the list and mark the ones who agreed to hear from you. Only those are invited: once each, when the last visit is more than three months old or has no date, and no visit is already booked. Your wording, a button to book, a limited number a day, and never a patient who said stop.

✗Without: your most valuable list sits in a spreadsheet, going colder
✓With: the ones you marked hear from your clinic once, politely
ENGINE 05 / 06

The next treatment, offered after the visit

When your reception marks a visit as attended, and you mapped a next treatment to the one they just had, the patient gets one note from your clinic with a button that starts booking it. Your protocols, your wording, never pushed.

✗Without: the patient leaves; the next visit is left to chance
✓With: the next step is one tap away, after a visit that happened
ENGINE 06 / 06

Each step counted

Patients who wrote in, visits booked, who came and who did not: counted on your page, read fresh each time you open it. Counts and times, and no money figure of ours. And the next unannounced shop still meets your clinic the way a patient does, with the engine answering, and grades what it finds, except how fast the first reply came: that reply is ours, so its speed is shown as the engine's own first-reply time, not graded as yours.

✗Without: "how is the front desk doing?" Nobody truly knows
✓With: one page, real counts, checked from outside

THE ESTATE · THREE THINGS FOR YOUR GOOGLE REPUTATION

Your Google front door, checked every day.

Where a new patient judges you before they ever write: your Google profile and your reviews. Checked, answered and asked for.

◈ THE ESTATE · ILLUSTRATION
ESTATE 01 / 03

Your Google profile, checked daily

Once a day the engine reads your Business Profile and checks four things: that the hours Google shows match the hours you gave us, that Google has not edited the profile on its own, that it does not show you as closed, and that the listing is in good standing. It changes nothing: you fix it in Google's own screen.

✗Without: wrong hours on the map result, found by a patient at a locked door
✓With: the engine checks every day, where today nobody does
ESTATE 02 / 03

Every review, a reply ready

When a review lands, the engine picks the reply your clinic approved for that star rating and sends it to your reception on WhatsApp: Post reply, or Don't reply. One tap and it is on Google within minutes. The engine adds only your clinic's name and phone: never the reviewer's name, never a treatment.

✗Without: the angry review sits unanswered for days, read by everyone who searches you
✓With: a reply is one tap away, in wording you already approved
ESTATE 03 / 03

Every attended patient asked for a review

A couple of hours after a visit your reception marked as attended, the patient gets one polite request with your Google review link. At most once a year. Every attended patient is asked, not only the happy ones: that is Google's rule, and we keep it.

✗Without: only the angry ones bother; your average sinks unfairly
✓With: every patient who came gets asked, once a year at most

HOW WE CONNECT, AND WHAT WE NEVER TOUCH

You grant access. You keep the keys.

No passwords, and no software installed at the clinic. Your WhatsApp number and your Google profile stay in your own accounts.

What we ask for

  • ✓WhatsApp: your number stays in your clinic's own Meta account; we connect to it on one call, through an official WhatsApp Business provider
  • ✓Google: you add us as a manager on your Business Profile, never as its owner
  • ✓The quiet list: only if you want the invitations: your past patients, with the ones who agreed marked
  • ✓Website: editor access, only if you want us to make the changes

What we never touch

  • ✗Your medical system: we never connect to it
  • ✗Your passwords: we never ask for one. Every connection is made from your own account, and your reception only taps inside WhatsApp
  • ✗Clinical questions: anything a patient types goes to your team; the engine answers only from the menu you approved
  • ✗Your money: the engine takes no payment; that stays between you and your patients
  • ✗Your ad accounts' billing: spend is paid direct, never marked up
What the engine keeps: each patient who messages your WhatsApp, every message both ways, the bookings, a record of what it did, and the quiet list you gave us. Stored encrypted. When you leave, a person here deletes it once 30 days have passed, and the backups a week after that.
